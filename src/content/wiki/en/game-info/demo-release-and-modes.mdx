## Can I play now?

Yes: the [free Demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5503600/WarFront_Demo/) is available as app **5503600**, linked to the [main WarFront product](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5344530/WarFront/), **5344530**. Developer and publisher are **Shu'la Lab LLC**. The checked main product remains Coming soon and lists **January 29, 2027** as a planned date, not a completed release.

Use the official Demo entry instead of treating the unreleased main app as a failed download. The Demo API lists November 5, 2026; the [download announcement](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1848664643344046) appeared November 6 at 03:14 UTC. Different date fields do not establish a delay.

## Which advertised content needs a boundary?

Read the specific announcement instead of applying every main-game tag to the Demo. The Demo announcement confirms commanding units, taking territory and building forces, but supplies no complete mode-by-mode entitlement table.

| Dead Front | The [November 3 announcement](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1847920302253054) plans February 5 after January 29. It is future mode information. |

The announced survival plane crash, scavenging and survivor systems must not become an invented walkthrough for today's RTS Demo. A campaign synopsis is not a mission solution, or proof that every advertised unit is unlocked.

## Do offline or multiplayer labels prove the join workflow?

No. Steam lists multiplayer categories while the store also describes offline play. These labels do not establish a verified invite sequence, player limit or identical access in every Demo mode. Check what the installed build actually offers; this site has not run online or local sessions.
