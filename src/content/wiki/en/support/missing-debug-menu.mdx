## Was the old menu supposed to remain?

The developer says it was included by accident and removed. In the [original menu discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5344530/discussions/0/568176317786499680/?l=english), a player describes removing production time, granting unlimited oil and spawning troops. A developer-badged reply at **November 8, 2026, 08:34 UTC** directly explains its disappearance.

That is more useful than guessing a hidden research requirement. No unlock quest, unit purchase or currency threshold is identified. Do not spend resources solely to recreate a menu the developer says was removed.

## Is there an official key to restore it?

None is confirmed in the inspected reply. This page does not supply console commands, file edits or a remembered cheat-key combination as supported current instructions. Seeing a menu in an earlier session is not proof a normal control is broken now.

The reply also does not settle every future sandbox feature. Keep this narrow removal fact separate from [Demo and full-game plans](/game-info/demo-release-and-modes/). It is not evidence that every future custom rule is impossible.

## What should I use instead to understand progression?

Follow the [published oil-delivery chain](/economy/oil-trucks-and-supply-routes/): controlling a rig and getting its trucks home are different tasks. Use [first-skirmish control checks](/start/first-skirmish-and-controls/) for unfamiliar orders. If a different normal control is missing, report that exact screen and the installed Demo, rather than assuming it is this removed menu.

Checked November 10, 2026. The removal is developer-sourced. Internal implementation and any future supported re-enable option remain unknown.
