## What is the supported first step?

Update the Demo, then compare the same visibility problem. In the [night-vision discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5344530/discussions/0/568176317786500408/?l=english), a developer-badged reply at **November 9, 2026, 19:43 UTC** says night-vision mode received an update and asks players to use the latest Demo. It gives no numeric build or slider value.

The original question concerned distinguishing forces at night. Being able to update or launch is not the same as confirming clear side identification. Check the colors or scene that caused your difficulty, keeping conditions comparable when documenting a remaining issue.

## Is disabling the day-night cycle an official fix?

No. An earlier participant says they usually disable it. That is a community choice, not a required developer remedy or proof the game cannot be played at night. The retained discussion does not verify the current option name, position or availability in every match type.

Do not convert this report into a universal brightness recommendation. No measured calibration, gamma value or hardware-specific preset is supplied. Separate a dark scene, hard-to-distinguish colors and an unresponsive control when reporting what remains wrong.

## What should a useful follow-up include?

Record the installed Demo/update state, nighttime scene, affected colors and changed settings. Say whether the problem remains after the developer-requested update. A screenshot of the present issue is more useful than a guessed build number or the assumption that an old complaint proves a new fix failed.

Use [control checks](/start/first-skirmish-and-controls/) for unfamiliar orders, [scouting and old sightings](/tactics/scouting-fog-and-old-sightings/) for stale information, and [launch help](/game-info/pc-requirements-and-launch-help/) if no match opens.

Checked November 10, 2026. This is a dated developer instruction with attributed player context, not a personal visibility test or a promise that every monitor and scenario is fixed.
