## Why is holding a rig not the whole income chain?

The resource still has a journey to complete. The [official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5344530/WarFront/) presents oil as the only money and describes rigs, trucks and roads carrying it home. It names shooting trucks, cutting roads and blowing bridges as ways to stop enemy oil arriving. A source marker alone is not a completed delivery.

## Which segment should I inspect first?

Follow the route instead of guessing a hidden production penalty. This is an editorial diagnosis checklist for the published physical-delivery model, not proof of automatic truck behavior in every Demo build.

| Segment | Useful question | Do not assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rig | Do I hold the source I rely on? | A nearby victory captured every object. |
| Truck | Can I see what happened to the vehicle? | Source ownership means delivered oil. |
| Road or bridge | Is the route intact and defended? | A broken crossing always has an automatic bypass. |
| Base | Has the transport actually reached home? | Oil appears remotely just because the rig is held. |

If trucks are repeatedly destroyed, capturing more rigs may leave the same bottleneck. More troops at the source do not themselves repair a broken road. Watch what actually fails before deciding what to protect.

## How do engineers and towns support the route?

The store names engineers cutting roads, bringing down bridges, digging trenches, blowing dams and fixing your own infrastructure. Before sabotage, consider which connection you depend on as well as the enemy's. These are planning checks, not verified clicks, costs or automatic repair rules.

Garrisoned towns are described as mending vehicles and making unit production faster. Inspect the actual town state rather than assuming a repair radius, required troop count or multiplier.

## What counts as progress or a useful failure report?

Check whether the intended transport gets home and delivery progresses, not merely whether another truck exists. Automatic dispatch, pathfinding rules, alternate-route selection and exact extraction rates remain unverified. Record the source, vehicle, damaged connection, destination and Demo version if behavior seems wrong.

Use [scouting and old sightings](/tactics/scouting-fog-and-old-sightings/) when the road ahead is no longer visible. For orders, compare [dated control reports](/start/first-skirmish-and-controls/). If unlimited oil disappeared, read the [removed-menu answer](/support/missing-debug-menu/) rather than treating it as an economy unlock.
