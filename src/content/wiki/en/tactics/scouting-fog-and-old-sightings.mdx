## What does a last-seen ghost tell me?

It records an earlier sighting, not a guaranteed current position. The [official store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5344530/WarFront/) says fog hides what your army cannot see and refers to ghosts of what was seen last. When visibility changes, separate an observed enemy from a reminder of its earlier location before committing forces.

![Original information-age checklist contrasting a current sighting and a last-seen marker, not a game HUD or measured radar range.](/images/articles/scouting-information-age.webp)

*This reading aid explains the published distinction; it is not a map of an actual match.*

## Which recon options are actually named?

The store names **scout cars, radar and a recon drone**. It does not give a complete vision table, radar cooldown, drone range or verified unlock sequence. Read the options available in your match rather than treating a named unit as one you already own.

Ask two questions: can I currently see the intended area, and which part of my plan relies on an older sighting still being true? Checking an exposed [oil road](/economy/oil-trucks-and-supply-routes/) is different from assuming a previously empty road remains empty. This planning check does not guarantee any tool reveals every hidden unit.

## Do generated maps or bots remove the need to scout?

No such shortcut is established. The store describes generated maps across four theatres and says bots use the same fog. Check the actual match rather than copying fixed coordinates from another map. That is not an exact fog algorithm, AI reaction-time measurement or proof that no special cases exist.

| Information | Safe interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| A unit is visible now | Use the current observation for the immediate decision. |
| A last-seen ghost remains | You know an earlier location, not a confirmed present one. |
| A recon tool is advertised | Verify its availability and behavior in this match. |
| A road was clear earlier | Recheck if the next decision depends on it remaining clear. |
